The Reality

Most candidates fail because they lose track, not because they aren't smart.

The CPA exam has a ~50% first-attempt pass rate across all four sections (source: AICPA/NASBA published pass rate data). The candidates who fail aren't unprepared. They run out of time, lose momentum, or have no way to tell whether their current pace will get them through their exam window.

Ledger gives you real visibility: exactly how many hours you've put in, how many you need, and whether you're on track for each section, each week, every day.

What You Get

Built to get you through
all four sections.

Ledger is a social study tracker first — everything here exists to keep you on pace, motivated, and connected to people doing the exact same work, so you don't run out of time or steam before you run out of sections.

Study alongside your people — so you actually keep showing up
Ledger is a social study tracker at heart. Log a session and it lands in a shared feed with your classmates, coworkers, or firm cohort. See who's grinding right now, cheer each other on, and keep each other going through the longest stretch of your career. Studying for the CPA is lonely and long — this is how you don't do it alone. It's encouragement and accountability, never a competition. Nobody's ranking you.
Core Feature
A Readiness Score that tells you the truth
One number, 0–100, built from your hours, your pace, your practice scores, and your consistency — so you know if you're actually ready or just busy. It updates every time you log a session, and if you're falling behind it tells you now, not the week before your exam.
Core Feature
A streak worth keeping, and a record of every win
Build a daily study streak and earn badges at every milestone. When you finally pass a section, Ledger marks the moment — the exact score, the exact hours it took, a card you can save. Years from now you'll still be able to pull up the day you passed FAR.
Core Feature
A day-by-day plan that bends when life happens
Set your exam date and Ledger builds a phase-by-phase study calendar — foundation, MCQ drilling, simulations, final sprint. Going on vacation or slammed at work? Block those days and your hours redistribute across the rest of your plan. No falling behind, no starting over.
Elite Feature
Scores, retakes, and your NTS countdown
Log every exam attempt — score, sit date, attempt number. Ledger auto-marks passed sections, and when you don't pass it shows your remaining NTS days and recalculates exactly how many hours you need before you sit again. Nothing slips through the cracks.
Core Feature
An AI coach that knows your study history
Ask Ledger anything — how does partnership basis work, which topics should I drill before Thursday, am I on pace before my NTS expires. The AI knows your hours, your sections, and your weak spots, so you get answers tailored to where you actually are.
AI responses may contain errors — always verify against your official review course before your exam.

Practice questions for every section, built in
When you want to drill, 1,900+ questions across all six sections are right there — full explanations on every choice, served at random. Pro adds 20 fresh AI questions per section daily.
