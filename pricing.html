FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?
No. The 7-day free trial for Pro and Elite requires no payment information. You only enter billing details if you decide to keep your subscription after the trial ends.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged. Cancel a paid subscription at any time, and you keep access through the end of your billing period.
What's the difference between Pro and Elite?
Pro covers everything most candidates need: the full question bank with 20 fresh AI questions per section every day, smart scheduling, advanced analytics, streak protection, and weekly progress insights. Elite unlocks Ask Ledger (AI coach), the day-by-day AI Study Plan, the unlimited AI question bank with custom topic generation, Study Partner Pairing, and the full Exam Simulator. If you're not sure, start with Pro.
What is the 6-Month Cram Plan?
It's a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that gives you full Elite access for 6 months, plus an exam-day protocol PDF and a personal milestone check-in. At $75 it's cheaper than 6 months of Elite billed monthly ($84) and includes extras. Designed for candidates with a fixed exam window.
Does the annual plan auto-renew?
Yes, annual plans renew automatically unless cancelled. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings.
Is there a student discount?
A student discount is coming soon. For now, Pro is $9/mo, already well below what other CPA tools charge. Stay tuned.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
