Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ledger collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Ledger is made by Momentum Apps LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), which is responsible for your information. We keep this plain.

1. What We Collect

Account information. Your email address, name, password (stored by Firebase Authentication; we never see it), and, if you sign in with Google, the name, email and photo your Google account shares. If you turn on two-step verification, we store its secret key.

Profile information. What you choose to add: profile photo, bio, school, class year, city and state, firm or study group, exam sections and dates, and passed sections.

Study data. Study sessions, hours, materials, notes, captions, session photos, streaks, goals, readiness scores, and exam scores you enter.

Social activity. Who you follow and who follows you, follow requests, props, comments, club and study-room membership, club chat messages, direct messages, and the people you block.

Reports. If you report someone or something, we keep the report, the reason, any details you add, and a copy of the reported text.

AI feature inputs. Questions and topics you send to the Ledger AI coach or AI study tools, and the responses.

Payment information. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never receive your full card number. We receive and keep your plan, subscription status, Stripe customer and subscription IDs, and billing dates.

Notification settings. If you turn on notifications, we keep a push token for your device, your time zone, and your reminder preferences.

Usage and device data. Pages visited, features used, device and browser type, approximate location (country or region), and IP address, collected through our hosting provider and, only if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics for Firebase.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to show you ads. We do not use your content to train AI models.

If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region, we rely on these legal bases: performing our contract with you (running the Service), our legitimate interests (safety, security and improving Ledger), your consent (analytics cookies and notifications, which you can withdraw at any time), and legal obligations.

3. What Other Users Can See

If your account is public, any signed-in Ledger user can see your name, photo, bio, school, location, firm, exam sections, total hours, streak, your session posts and their captions, props and comments. Exam scores are shown only if you turn that on. If your account is private, only followers you approve see your activity, though your name and photo still appear in search and in clubs you join.

Club chat messages are visible to that club's members; firm cohorts and public clubs can be joined by others. Direct messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Comments are visible to anyone who can see the post. You can mark individual sessions private and control what's shared in Settings → Privacy.

4. Who We Share It With

We share personal information only with service providers who process it for us under contract, and as described here:

  • Google (Firebase): sign-in, database, push notifications, and, with your consent, analytics. Data is stored in the United States.
  • Stripe: payments and billing. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic: processes what you send to AI features to produce responses.
  • Resend: sends our emails (the weekly recap, and reports to our moderators).
  • Vercel: hosts the website and app servers, and provides privacy-friendly page analytics that don't use cookies.

We may also share information if the law requires it (for example a valid subpoena or court order), to protect the safety of users or the public, to enforce our Terms, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Ledger stores your sign-in session and preferences in your browser's local storage; the app can't work without these. Google Analytics for Firebase is used only if you accept the cookie banner, and you can change your answer at any time with the "Cookie Settings" link at the bottom of every page or in the app's Settings → Privacy. Vercel's page analytics don't use cookies. We don't use advertising cookies.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Private account and sharing. Settings → Privacy lets you make your account private, hide your hours, streak and scores, stop sharing activity, and turn off messages.

Block and report. You can block anyone and report content from its ⋯ menu.

Export. Download your study data as a file from Settings at any time, or email us for a full copy of your personal information.

Correct or delete. Edit your profile at any time, or delete your account in Settings.

Email and notifications. Unsubscribe from the weekly recap with the link in any recap email or in Settings → Notifications. You can turn off push notifications in Settings or on your device. Account and billing messages can't be turned off while you have an account.

Depending on where you live (for example California, other U.S. states, the EU or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent. Email support@ledgercpa.app to make a request. We'll verify it's you, answer within the time the law requires, and won't treat you differently for using these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. How Long We Keep It

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, study data, followers, follow requests, comments on your posts, props, blocks and notification settings right away, and your sign-in. Copies in service-provider logs or backups may last up to 30 days.

Some things stay after you delete your account: comments you left on other people's posts and messages you sent in chats remain in those conversations (email us and we'll remove them), reports are kept as long as needed to keep the community safe and handle disputes, and Stripe and we keep billing records as long as tax and accounting law requires. We may keep information longer if the law requires it or to deal with abuse.

8. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (TLS), database security rules that limit who can read and write each record, optional two-step verification, and Stripe for all card handling. Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers but are not end-to-end encrypted. No system is perfectly secure. If you find a security issue, please email support@ledgercpa.app.

9. Children

Ledger is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email support@ledgercpa.app and we'll delete it.

10. Where Your Data Is Processed

Ledger is run from the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use Ledger from another country, your information is transferred to the U.S., where privacy laws may differ. Where required, we rely on our providers' standard contractual clauses or other approved safeguards for these transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Ledger changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes we'll tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.
